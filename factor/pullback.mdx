---
title: "2. 回踩辅助线（昨日回踩、今日重启）"
sidebarTitle: "回踩"
description: "当天收盘标记。原文："
---

```
MID:=(3*CLOSE+LOW+OPEN+HIGH)/6;
牛线:=(20*MID+19*REF(MID,1)+...+2*REF(MID,18)+REF(MID,20))/210;
生命线:=MA(牛线,5);
Cond_Trend := 牛线>生命线 AND 牛线>=REF(牛线,1);
Cond_Yest_Dip := REF(LOW,1)<=REF(牛线,1)*(1+0.015);
Today_Stand := CLOSE>牛线*(1+0.005);
Cond_Today_Up := CLOSE>REF(CLOSE,1);
Cond_Today_Yang := CLOSE>OPEN;
XG: Cond_Trend AND Cond_Yest_Dip AND Today_Stand AND Cond_Today_Up AND Cond_Today_Yang;
```

牛线和生命线与 [上升通道](/factor/rising-channel) 相同。五个条件都要成立。昨日条件读 `i-1`，所以至少要有上一根 K 线，并且上一根牛线有效。生命线从下标 24 起有效。
